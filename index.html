Dr. Strange

Doctor Strange is a Marvel Comics superhero created by Steve Ditko and Stan Lee, who serves as the Sorcerer Supreme and Earth's protector against mystical threats.



Character Overview

• Real Name: Dr. Stephen Vincent Strange

• First Appearance: Strange Tales #110 (July 1963)

• Abilities: Mystic arts, astral projection, mirror dimension manipulation, time control, and interdimensional travel.

• Live-Action Portrayal: Played by Benedict Cumberbatch in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU).



Main MCU Films

• Doctor Strange (2016): An arrogant neurosurgeon ruins his hands in a car crash and travels to Kamar-Taj in Nepal, where he learns the mystic arts from the Ancient One.

• Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness (2022): Strange traverses alternate realities to protect America Chavez from the Scarlet Witch.
